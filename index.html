Country Jai · Artist profile

Country Jai weaves real stories into modern country tracks – the kind you belt out on a long drive or keep on repeat when you need a lift. Blending classic influences with a chart-ready sound, the music draws on family, friends and the highs and lows of life in the UK.

Country Jai burst onto the scene in early 2025 with his debut single STOP! This recieved widespread acclaim from Music crittics in the UK and afar! Going on to hit 100,000 Streams in just the first 3 months and momentum well and truely on his side Jai released his first album 'LITTLE BIT DIFFERENT' featuring fan favourites 'One Time Hit!' and 'Play to Win'

Jai cemented his place on the UK Country Scene releasing his cover of 'PINK PONY CLUB' which has been streamed worldwide.

November 2025 saw Country Jai release 'THE SILLY, NAUGHTY ELF' raising funds for The Saints Foundation, this single went on to be used in hundreds of reels on social media racking up more than 4 MILLION views on Instagram alone! From heartfelt ballads to crowd-ready anthems, every song is built around one idea: if it matters to real people, it belongs in a song.
